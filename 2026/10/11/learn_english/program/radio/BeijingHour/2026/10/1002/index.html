But now I’m very comfortable in China too, and so, you know, even now we go back every summer to China for our summer holiday. We have an apartment in Guangzhou that we keep, and so I, you know, we go back and forth between the two countries, and, you know, it really feels like I have a second home.

And so, I would say that that came very early for me, because again, you know, the Chinese people are so friendly. You know, when you can speak the language, it’s so much fun to interact with the locals, you know, eat the Chinese cuisine. You know, I can eat Chinese food every day. You know, I can live like a local. I mean, I really enjoy it. So, to me, it very naturally became my second home, and I just really embraced it, I really loved it.

Now, I wanna talk about your YouTube channel of yours. Now you have, like, over a million subscribers already. I actually watched some of the videos you posted there, like before this interview. And a lot of the content is really fascinating, I mean the videos, you sort of talk about the differences between what China really is and how it’s portrayed in media. Like, what made you decide to pick up a camera and, you know, start talking in front of a camera?

You know, it’s actually a very interesting story, because it happened by accident. You know, I actually started a YouTube channel about golf, and so I was making golf videos and just having fun and, you know, tapping into my very big golf experience.

But what I realized is this: I thought to myself, you know, a lot of people can make golf videos, but not many westerners can really talk about China. Not many westerners have had the opportunity to live in China, to, you know, build a career there, to really understand China. So I thought to myself, you know, I know a lot about China, probably more than most westerners do. So I have this unique opportunity to speak about China.

And there was this particular instance, it was in the end of 2019, LeBron James, the basketball player, he came to China, and there was a very big controversy happening then. And I said, you know, I’m gonna make a video about this. And actually with the Hong Kong protest that was happening, and I thought, you know, I need to share my perspective about the Hong Kong protest because what we’re hearing in western media is not accurate of what’s actually happening on the ground in Hong Kong. And I, you know, I said, you know, I think one of my first videos was, you know, about like why this American does not support the Hong Kong protest, and why there’s a lot more to the story.

And so, you know, I posted a video, and it was very well received. You know, a lot of Chinese people really enjoyed it. A lot of, you know, foreigners as well said, wow, thank you for sharing your perspective, Cyrus. Can you make a video about this topic? And I said, sure, I can make a video about that.

So I made a second video, and then a third, and then very quickly we started to gain some followers, and I guess that was very early to the game. You know, I was one of the first westerners to come on to YouTube to start making videos about China, sharing my perspective.

And so, I quickly thought to myself, I said, you know, if I can get to 10,000 subscribers, if I can get to 10,000, I think I’m gonna take that as a sign that people enjoy this content, and that there’s a real opportunity here. Because if I get to 10,000, I think I can get to 100,000. If I get to 100,000, I think I get to a million. But the first goal for me was to get to 10,000, and I really used that as a benchmark to understand if there is enough interest and people.

You know, we had amazing support. People from around the world were watching the videos, and I think that’s really the power of YouTube, that’s the power of the internet, is that you can connect with people all over the world, and so we have people in Africa, in Europe, in Asia, North America, literally all over the world, tuning in, and I thought, wow, this is really incredible, I’m very, you know, blessed to have this opportunity, and I’m gonna keep working hard on this.

And so, it shifted from a hobby into, you know, something I really wanna pursue full time, and I really want to, you know, make it a career, and really make the best videos I can about China. And so that’s really how the YouTube journey started.

You said people accuse you, you know, although groundlessly, really, of being paid by the government to say things are good about China, but actually something really happened to you, right? You recently mentioned that someone tried to offer you 10,000 US dollars to lie to your audience about China. What exactly happened there?

Yeah, it was very interesting. So, it was actually a company that reached out to me, and they said, Cyrus, you know, we’d like to sponsor one of your videos. The video would be about the AI race between the United States and China. Would you be willing to, you know, do a sponsorship? And I said, you know, we do have sponsors on our channel, so that is one of the sources of revenue that we do have on the channel. And I said, please tell me more about your company and, you know, what is the promotion?

And so they said, well, the budget is 10,000, and this is what you need to say in the video. You need to say, everybody, there’s a race going on right now between the United States and China, and I want everybody to know that I am Team USA. We must win the race. China cannot win. USA’s number one.

And this was the interesting part. I said, well, who’s the company? And who’s paying for this? And they said, well, this has to be anonymous. You know, we cannot, we can’t disclose, you know, who’s paying for this. And I said, well, I don’t do sponsorships like that, because I don’t know where the money is coming from, and I don’t know who’s behind this message. But I also thought it was very interesting, because why would you pay 10,000 if I’m not gonna promote your company, right?

If I’m not going to say, you know, for example, if it was an American AI company, and, you know, maybe it’s something simple like, hey, we’ve just launched this new AI tool. If you’d like to try it out, you can click the link down below, and you can register today. You know, there needs to be a way that you can track that information, that way that you know it’s a profitable campaign. This is a normal type of sponsorship.

But this was a very big red flag to me, because they said, well, there’s no call to action, there’s no promotion of a company, you don’t tell me where the money is coming from. This does not seem right to me, and I don’t do things for money. You know, I don’t just, I’m not going to just say these words and get paid money to do that, because I am an independent reporter. I, you know, make money on YouTube, you know, obviously YouTube pays its creators through ads. And, you know, there are many avenues that we can monetize the YouTube channel.

So, I actually rejected that, but then talked about it, and I just wanted more people to understand. Look, this is what’s happening right now, and we know that there is a big AI race between the United States and China. But, again, you know, there are great advances from both countries as well. You know, both countries have great AI researchers, both countries are producing great results.

And so one of the things you’ll often hear me talk about on my channel is how does the United States and China work together? Because if the United States and China work together, then the entire world is going to win. That’s my mission, that’s my goal, my goal is to always foster a better relationship and a more integrated relationship between the United States and China.

I love both countries. I’m a proud American. I will always love the United States. It is my home country, but I also will love China, and there’s nothing wrong with that. I am pro China, I’m also pro America, and I’m pro both countries working together.

Yeah, but do you think we are really heading towards, you know, two separate technology ecosystems? Or do you think that at the end of the day we’ll have to, you know, pick sides? Where do you think the two countries will eventually really settle down and drop their differences and agree to work together?

I honestly think that it’s imp ple and to really, you know, separate that relationship. You know, we look at our quality of life in America. The, you know, American dream that we live here is made possible because of China.

You know, we look at simply all the things in our house. If I were to take you on a tour in my house, and to show you the things that I have here in the United States, a large portion of it comes directly from China. I mean, simply the camera that I’m speaking to right now, the laptop that I’m using, you know, the microphones, the headphones, all of this comes from China, or it has parts that come from China.

So, it’s impossible for me to, you know, remove China from my life. I think it’s impossible for every American to remove China from their life. So, we have to understand that, you know, China makes incredible products, and as a result of that, we’re able to enjoy those products at a very good price point, and we’re actually able to live a better quality of life.

And so I think that this is an amazing thing, and there’s so much trade going on between the two countries. And I always say this: if you listen to United States politicians, they talk a very big game.

You know, we don’t need China, decouple from China, China is our enemy. But in the real world, in the business world, what do actual businessmen do? They actually go to China.

You look at, you know, for example, look at many American companies that are very active in the China market. Why? Because it’s an incredible market. Why wouldn’t you want to sell your products to a market of 1.4 billion people? You look at Starbucks, you know, for many, many years, their biggest growth was coming from China.

Again, it’s very important to understand: politicians will say one thing, but business leaders, they will always say the truth, because they are voting with their wallets, and at the end of the day, they have to do business. And we know that doing business with China is a good thing.

Now, you said that if you want to understand this global conversation about China, you have to be physically inside the country. Why is that?

Yeah, absolutely. I think what’s important, and what I mean by that, is that a lot of the biggest critics that I’ve seen, you know, that are very anti-China, have never spent a day inside the country. And I find that to be very strange to me, because how can you be confident about what is going on in China, or how can you talk about China, if you’ve never visited the country?

And this is, ironically, what many US politicians do. They try to lecture the world about China, yet they have not spent a single day in China, they have never spoken to an actual Chinese person in China. And so, I think what’s amazing is that I have yet to meet a single person that has gone to China and not come back a totally different person, and just has a different perspective.

Because, you know, in our media, for example, we portray China as this totalitarian state. You know, nobody in China has freedom. Everybody in China is, you know, ruled by the government, and there’s a huge fear of the government in China. But, you know, I always tell people, I said, you know, there is something that I’d want you to know about China: right now in China, people are, you know, going to a restaurant, and they’re gonna have dinner with their friends. They’re going to watch the latest movie that’s coming out. People are at a coffee shop getting a cup of coffee. You know, there’s a grandmother walking her granddaughter to school in the morning.

You know, Chinese people are very similar to American people. Actually, I think that’s very similar everywhere around the world. We all want the same things, right? We all want a good job, we’d like to have a family. We hope that our children can live a better life than we get to live, and so that’s the same values and the same goals whether you’re in America or whether you’re in China.

People are working hard, they’re doing their jobs, they’re taking holidays. You know, they are living their lives in China, and so I think it’s actually very interesting. You know, for example, when I was in Guangzhou this summer, there was a beautiful sports complex with about 25 basketball courts, and they’re all brand new. You know, there’s probably thousands of Chinese people playing basketball in the evenings. And I made a very simple video on Instagram, and I just shared it. There was about a 10 second video, I just panned the courts. And I said, look at what the government is building here for the people.

And the video went viral. You know, people were like, wow, I never could imagine that China would have basketball courts. I never knew that people could play basketball in China. I thought that the Chinese government would not allow basketball to happen. And I thought, wow, that’s very crazy to think that. But again, this is why a simple video of a basketball court can change people’s perspective.

They said, Cyrus, this is amazing! Not only are there 25 beautiful basketball courts, but look at the beautiful buildings around. There’s skyscrapers everywhere. It’s modern, there’s LED lights, there’s screens, there’s EV, it looks very high tech. It’s so much more advanced than I ever would have imagined. And so, there’s just a lot of power in showing that.

You know, I was recording another video in the morning, and, you know, in China, a lot of elderly people, they go to the streets in the morning, they’re doing tai chi. So, I’m recording a video, and I purposely set my camera up, and behind me there’s 50 elderly people doing tai chi at about 6:30, 7:00 o’clock in the morning. This is very common in China. And they said, wow, this is amazing, like Chinese people just go to the street, they just exercise in the street. I said yes, and then if you come back in the evening, a lot of them are dancing. You know, there’s a lot of dancing going on in the street. This is how Chinese people get their exercise.

And so, for many Americans, they find this very fascinating. They said, this is incredible, you know, this is actually a very healthy society. I said yes, it is, it is actually a very healthy society. You know, the average lifespan in China is longer than the average American lifespan. I think a lot of that is the diet, you know, very healthy diet, very active lifestyle. You know, as we age, we have to stay active.

So it’s just sharing little things like that, which I find is so fascinating. You know, it’s very easy to share, but I also find it very helpful, because it helps more people understand that China is a very normal society, you know, no different than many other countries in the world.

After all these years living in both countries, what do you think are some of the notable things that ordinary Chinese and Americans often get wrong about each other?

Well, I think for most Americans, they believe that China doesn’t have any freedom. And so that would be the first thing that you hear, you know, for many Americans. And so I give them the examples that I just gave, you know, the freedom to have safety. And, in my opinion, I think safety is really the top freedom. You know, I mean, I want to live in a very safe society, and I think that is something that is very amazing about China, but it’s also amazing about Asia. You know, generally speaking, most of Asia is very safe, and so there’s a much higher level of safety. You know, similar if you’re in Japan or Thailand. Generally speaking, it’s a very safe place to be.

And so, you know, in America, for example, you know, we value having guns. You know, this is actually the Second Amendment of the United States. It is the right to bear arms. And so for many Americans, they would actually argue that they would say, well, I would rather live in a more unsafe society while I’m able to own a gun. I would actually give up some safety to have the freedom of having a gun. But I’ve talked to many Chinese people, and their viewpoint is the complete opposite. They would say, I’m happy to live in a society with no guns, because as a result, it’s going to be a lot more safe.

So again, neither side is wrong, it’s just a difference in culture. And I understand that. I’m an American, I mean I understand why Americans are like that, because our country was founded by people grabbing their guns and fighting off the British, right? You know, the redcoats are coming. You know, this is the message that was here. You know, grab your gun, protect your country, protect your family, fight for your freedom. So, our nation was literally built by grabbing a gun and standing up for your freedom.

Meanwhile in China, this is not how the nation was started, and this is not how the nation has developed. So again, it’s a different cultural perspective, and I think that is really, really important to understand.

Now, if you could only put one message to, you know, people in China and the US, what would that be?

My message would be that we are more similar than we are different, and so we need to work, you know, we need to identify where we’re similar. And we need to figure out how we can always work together. And then, exactly what I just mentioned, is that, you know, we only have one world to live in, and, you know, for example, when we look at something like climate change, this is going to need the United States and China collaborating together. As the two major superpowers on the world, you’re not going to be able to solve the issue of climate change unless you have both countries working together. If China does it alone, it’s not gonna be enough. If the United States does it alone, it’s not going to be enough. But if China and the United States really work together on this, it could make a major difference for the world.

And so I think when you look at establishing peace in the Asian Pacific region, and really around the world, you know, there’s a great opportunity for the US and China to, you know, work together.

Kudos to you sir, I’m just really grateful, you know, we have someone like you trying to dissolve all these differences and misunderstandings, clearing up, you know, a lot of misunderstandings between China and the West. Yeah, keep up the good work.
