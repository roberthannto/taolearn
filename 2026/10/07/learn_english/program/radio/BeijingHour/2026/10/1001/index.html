So I was very happy that I got to show them what China is really like, as opposed to what some propaganda says about it. So they really loved it, and like me, they love the food, they love hot pot.

Right, glad to know that. So Penny, what has Jonathan told you about America that you find really interesting, that you wouldn’t have known by simply reading books about the plays or you know?

I think it’s something related to family. Cause still a stereotype for Americans. So there was a rumor said once you pass 18, and you’re on your own, like your parents wouldn’t take care of you anymore in America. And from what I’ve seen with my eyes, truly, in Jonathan’s family, it was not true, it’s not true at all. Like Jonathan’s parents actually take care of our grandparents very carefully. They actually live together, and they take care of the grandparents every single day for years. And also their siblings, like Johnson has three siblings together. And they care for each other, they talk to each other, they video call each other. Wow. So yeah, I think it was not completely true that American family members are not bonded with each other. At least not for all of them.

For me, like I mentioned a little bit, meeting her mother for the first time was really scary. OK, I need to do this, I need to do that. I need to buy these gifts. Yeah, I think that’s true with some families.

I wouldn’t say it was hard. But when I met Jonathan’s parents for the first time in America, not only parents, like siblings, parents, grandparents. Even his parents’ friends, cousin, everyone. Yeah.

They had no problems with it, none at all. I don’t think anyone really cared that much. Or, I don’t want to say didn’t care, but they were happy for me. They weren’t worried about that at all. My parents were pretty surprised when I told them that I was dating a foreigner. Cause they were always expecting a Chinese boy for me. Yeah. So um. But actually, later on, I’ve talked about this with my mother. And she told me that like years ago, she had one fortune teller. You know, I don’t know. Pretty often, I’d say. OK. They live in Huainan, it’s not too far away. Two and a half hours by train. Yeah, so we go there like every National Day, May Day and Spring Festival.

OK, yep, so it’s pretty easy.

I do try to talk to them a lot after class and in class, of course. I’m an English teacher, so I try to talk to them a lot and try to get them to talk. I think so. There’s one particular thing that I would just like to talk about. At the beginning of the time that we dated together, we went out, for example, for lunch or dinner with friends. Mm hmm. Right. Oh yeah, I’ve learned the true secrets, like pretend you need to go to the bathroom and then pay the bill. Oh, I do that all the time as well. That’s kind of the social obligation, I pay this time, you pay that time and next time. So I don’t have that. So it’s something I had to get used to here.

Let’s see. Well, everyone compliments me on this. My chopstick skills are much better now.
